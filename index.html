ROOTED IN HISTORY. BUILT FOR WHAT'S NEXT.

The next chapter
of industry.

Historic industrial buildings once helped change how America worked. We're giving these remarkable places a new purpose—creating flexible workspaces for a new generation of ideas and enterprise.

The Phoenix Mill building was one of Henry Ford's factories that he built in the small towns and villages. This fully restored factory, that once hosted Ford Model T and Model A part production and Rosie the Riveters making parts for WWII, is the perfect place to provide inspiration while you work.

The Village Industry is an independent workspace brand inspired by industrial heritage.
